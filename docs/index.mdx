Brume is a native Android app for living with tinnitus: sounds to blend with it by day and to fall asleep to at night, paced breathing and short meditations, and a journal of one check-in a day. It is a wellness app, not a medical device: it doesn't replace a doctor, an ENT or an audiologist, and its About screen lists when to see one.

## 🧭 Finding your way

Home is one card per activity: **Sleep**, **Settle**, **Breathe**, **Meditate**, **Tones**, **My tinnitus**, **Journal** and **Learn**. Settings sit behind the quiet icon. Under **Settings › Activities** you can switch Breathe, Meditate and Tones off; they leave the home screen and nothing is deleted.

**Learn** holds short cards on what research says about tinnitus, each marked with how well it is supported (well supported, mixed, little evidence, or myth) and naming its sources. Beside them sit a decibel dial (how loud, for how long), a deck of kind tips, and where to turn for help, with numbers for France, the United Kingdom or elsewhere, chosen by the country you live in rather than the language.

The app speaks English and French, and from 22:00 to 06:00 it goes dark and dim whatever the system theme.
