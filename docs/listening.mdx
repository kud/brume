Brume's sounds are meant to sit just under your tinnitus so the two blend, the sound still just there. Covering it completely isn't needed.

## 🌙 Sleep and Settle

Both open the same kind of screen, a mix of sounds with one play disc:

- **Sleep** — to fall asleep, with a timer. Empty, it suggests brown noise with rain, stopping after 45 minutes.
- **Settle** — your mix by day, no timer. Empty, it suggests pink noise with the sea.

A suggestion only plays when you tap it, and you can choose other sounds at any time.

## 🎚️ The mixer

Layer as many sounds as you like, each at its own level:

| Kind | Sounds |
| --- | --- |
| Noises | White (bright and even), pink (softer, balanced), brown (deep and low), grey (even to the ear) |
| Recordings | Rain, rain on a terrace, storm in a tent, wind, sea, stream, birds, summer night, fireplace, café, night train |

Noises are generated on the phone. The recordings are CC0 field recordings, credited in About. Each layer also has a **Tone** setting, from soft to bright, with even in the middle.

Sliding up or down on the screen moves every layer by the same step, so the blend keeps its shape. The volume is told in words (very soft, soft, medium, loud, very loud) rather than a number.

## 🛡️ Listening safely

- Every start fades in from silence over about three seconds.
- Brume never changes your phone's volume. Only you do.
- **Too loud** stops everything at once, and the next start is about 6 dB quieter.
- A resumed sound never comes back louder than it was.
- Playback pauses when the headphones or speaker change, or when another app starts playing.

## ⏳ Sleep timer

One tap steps through off, 20, 45 and 90 minutes. The sound fades out over the timer's last five minutes.

## 🔈 Output and balance

**Settings › Sound output** shows where the sound plays (phone speaker, wired, Bluetooth or USB headphones, hearing aids) and lets you change it. **Balance** turns the other side down, never this one up, and is kept for each output.

Playback carries on with the screen off, from a media notification.
