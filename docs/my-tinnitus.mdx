Both are optional: the comfort sounds don't need a pitch.

## 🔍 Find the sound you hear

**My tinnitus** is a path of about five minutes, every step skippable, that stays on the phone:

1. **Good conditions** — a quiet room, wired or closed headphones if you have them, one ear at a time.
2. **Which ear** — left, right or both.
3. **Start from silence** — you raise the volume yourself until the sound is soft and comfortable. **Too loud** cuts to silence at once.
4. **Whistle or hiss** — which is closer to yours.
5. **Roughly how high** — steps every half-octave from 250 Hz up to about 11,300 Hz.
6. **Closer** — a fine dial around your choice.
7. **Lower, same or higher** — an octave either side, since many people first land a step away.
8. **In your words** — where you hear it (an ear, both, in the head) and whether it is steady, comes and goes, or keeps time with your heartbeat.
9. **Today** — how loud and how much it bothers you, if you want to say.
10. **A name** — so you can tell profiles apart.

If what you describe matches a sign worth raising (in time with your heartbeat, one ear only, sudden onset), Brume says it is worth mentioning to a doctor, calmly.

Each profile keeps every search, showing the range between them, since the pitch moves a little from day to day. You can search again, keep several profiles, add a comfort sound shaped around the pitch, or remove a profile.

## 🎵 The tone pattern

**Tones** plays a tone pattern in the style of ACRN: four soft tones around the pitch you set, in a shifting order, three beats on and two off. It is a practice in timed sessions, on its own: starting it stops the other sounds, and playing a sound stops it.

- **Pitch** — 500 to 14,000 Hz, 4,000 Hz to begin with. The **Match tone** plays a steady tone at the pitch instead of the pattern, to compare.
- **Tone level** — starts lower than the noises. Keep it just audible.
- **Balance** — evens out the four tones so none stands out.

The app is plain about it: studies on tone patterns like this one are few and small, so try it if you're curious and stop if it doesn't suit you.
