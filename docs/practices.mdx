title: 🌬️ Breathe and meditate
description: The breathing rhythms and meditation sessions in Brume, their lengths and how they guide you.
---

Both practices can be followed with the eyes closed: a light vibration or a soft bell marks each change, and an optional voice, spoken by the phone and offline, says the steps while the words always show on screen.

## 🌬️ Breathe

Five rhythms, each with a pace and a length:

| Rhythm | What it is | Pace | Length |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Long out-breath | About six breaths a minute, lying down or sitting | Beginner 3–5, standard 4–6, or with a hold | 3, 5, 10, 15 or 20 min |
| Cyclic sigh | Two breaths in through the nose, a long sigh out through the mouth | Standard | 3, 5 or 10 min |
| Humming | A soft hum on the out-breath, mouth closed, seated | Beginner or standard | 5, 7, 9 or 11 rounds |
| Box | In, hold, out, hold; seated | Beginner 4-2-4-2 or standard 4-4-4-4 | 1, 3, 5 or 10 min |
| 4-7-8 | In through the nose, a hold, a long out-breath through the mouth; seated | Beginner 2-3.5-4 or standard 4-7-8 | 4 cycles at most |

A session counts whole breaths and never cuts one short. **Sound underneath** fades in the sound you chose in Listen, if you like.

Humming and 4-7-8 begin with a short **Before you start**. Wherever a rhythm holds the breath, Brume says to hold only to the first light urge to breathe, to stop if you feel dizzy or unwell, never in water or while driving, and to talk to a health professional first if you are pregnant, have a heart problem or panic attacks.

## 🧘 Meditate

| Session | What it does | Length |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Body | Attention moves slowly from the feet to the face | 3, 5 or 10 min |
| Sounds | Near and far sounds, the one in the ears among them | 3, 5 or 10 min |
| Breath | Rest on the breath, and come back to it when the mind wanders | 3, 5 or 10 min |
| Timer | No guide: a bell at the start and at the end | 5, 10, 15 or 20 min |

A guided session runs arrive, the anchor, then stretches of silence that grow longer, then a close, with a soft bell at each step. The screen dims once a session starts and brightens again just before it ends. You can stop at any time.
